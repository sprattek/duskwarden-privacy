Duskwarden Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Duskwarden is a mobile game made by Matej Drlička, Slovakia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game sends over the internet, why, and what you can do about it. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your heroes stay on your phone; finished runs go to the online ladder unless you turn that off.

Who is responsible

Matej Drlička, Slovakia, is the controller of the data described here. Contact: support@firespot.app.

What stays on your phone

Your heroes, their looks, gear, talents and progress, your settings and your local ladder are saved only on your device. We never receive them, except for what is listed below.

What the game sends

When a run ends and "Share my runs online" is on (it is on by default), the game sends:

DataWhyWho can see it
A random player id made on your phone the first time a run goes onlineTo group your runs and heroes, and to delete them when you askOnly us. The public ladder shows a scrambled form of it (a hash) so that heroes with the same name can be told apart
Hero name, class, race, game mode, level, paragon level, score and a short summary of the best runThe public online ladderEveryone who plays the game
Run details: zone, difficulty, victory, time, kills, guardians, boss, run level, skills, talents, gear and frame rateTo balance the game and find problemsOnly us
Game version and your phone's model name (for example "Pixel 8")To find bugs that happen on certain devices or versionsOnly us

Like any internet service, our database host also sees your device's IP address when the game connects. We don't store it with your runs and don't use it to find out who or where you are.

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising id or any other identifier from your phone. Pick a hero name that doesn't reveal who you are: it is shown publicly on the ladder. Names with offensive words can't be used.

Purchases

If you buy the full game, Google Play or the App Store handles the payment. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made, never your payment details. Their own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Legal basis

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running the online ladder and in improving the game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning off "Share my runs online".

Where the data is kept

The data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase processes it only on our behalf.

How long we keep it

  • Run details for balancing: up to 24 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Ladder entries: until you delete the hero or your online data, or until we reset the ladder.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing: on the hero list, open Privacy (or Ladders) and turn off "Share my runs online". Runs that haven't been sent yet are dropped.
  • Delete a hero's online data: deleting a hero in the game also deletes its runs and ladder entries online.
  • Delete all your online data: on the hero list, open Privacy and tap "Delete my online data". It goes through as soon as your phone is online.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything on your phone. Your online data stays until you delete it or it expires as above, so delete it in the game first if you want it gone.
  • Step-by-step: how to delete your data.
  • You also have the right to access, correct, restrict or receive a copy of your data, and to object to its processing. Write to us with your player id, which you'll find under Privacy in the game.
  • If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Slovakia, that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky, dataprotection.gov.sk).

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data that identifies a child. Because the game has no accounts and asks for no contact details, the only personal detail a player can enter is a hero name. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a hero name, write to us and we'll delete it.

Terms of Service

The rules for playing the game, purchases and the online ladder are in the Terms of Service.

Changes

If the game starts sending other data (for example cloud saves through Google Play Games or Game Center), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
