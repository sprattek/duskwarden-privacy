Delete your Duskwarden data

Duskwarden, made by Matej Drlička, Slovakia

Duskwarden has no accounts. Your heroes and progress are saved only on your phone. The only data we keep is what the game sends online: your finished runs and ladder entries, saved under a random player id made on your phone. You can delete it in the game, or ask us to delete it.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Duskwarden. On the hero list, tap Privacy at the bottom.
  2. Tap Delete my online data and confirm with Delete.
  3. All runs and ladder entries this phone has sent are deleted, and sharing turns off. If the phone is offline, the deletion goes through as soon as it is online again.

To delete only one hero's online data, delete that hero on the hero list: its runs and ladder entries are deleted with it.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't use the game anymore (for example, you uninstalled it), write to support@firespot.app with the subject "Duskwarden data deletion". If you still have the game, add your player id from Privacy in the game, or the name of your hero as it shows on the online ladder, so we can find your data. We delete it within 30 days and write back when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your ladder entries: hero names, classes, races, levels and scores.
  • Your run details: zones, times, skills, talents, gear, game version and phone model.
  • The link between them and your player id.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Data on your phone (heroes, progress, settings) is deleted when you uninstall the game.
