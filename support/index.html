Duskwarden Support

Duskwarden, made by Matej Drlička, Slovakia

Need help? Write to support@firespot.app. Tell us your phone model and what happened; if you can, add your player id from Privacy on the hero list. We usually answer within a few days.

You can also send feedback right from the game: Send feedback at the bottom of the hero list.

I got a new phone. How do I get my heroes back?

Your heroes are saved on your phone and, if you're signed in, in your own cloud save: Google Play Games on Android, iCloud on iPhone. Install the game on the new phone with the same Google or Apple account and open it: your heroes come back on their own. Under Privacy on the hero list, the game says whether your heroes are saved to the cloud; on Android you can sign in to Play Games there. Cloud saves don't move between Android and iPhone.

I bought the full game, but it's locked

  • Open Get the full game on the hero list and tap Restore purchase.
  • Make sure the phone uses the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with, and that it's online.
  • A purchase that waits for payment unlocks as soon as the store confirms it.

Still locked? Write to us. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their rules.

My hardcore hero died. Can I get them back?

No. A hardcore hero has one life, and leaving or closing the game during a run counts as a fall. A cloud save never brings a fallen hero back either. Classic heroes can't die for good.

Someone on the online ladder has an offensive name

Tap the flag next to their score and confirm Report. The player disappears from your ladder right away and we review the name; offensive names are removed. To see hidden players again, use Privacy on the hero list.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your Duskwarden data. In short: Privacy on the hero list, then Delete my online data. Cloud saves are deleted in the Google Play Games or iCloud settings.

The game runs slowly on my phone

Pause a run and tap Effects: full to switch to Effects: light: fewer damage numbers and effects, smoother play on older phones.
